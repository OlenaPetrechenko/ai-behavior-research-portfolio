Based on your preferences, I'd recommend **avoiding Santorini and Mykonos**. September is a wonderful time to visit Greece because the sea is still warm, the weather is pleasant (24–29°C), and many destinations are much quieter than in August.

### My top recommendation: **Pelion Peninsula**

Pelion offers a perfect balance of nature, traditional villages, secluded beaches, and excellent local food.

**Why it matches your preferences:**

* Quiet beaches such as Mylopotamos and Fakistra
* Traditional mountain villages like Tsagarada and Makrinitsa
* Scenic hiking trails through forests
* Family-run tavernas with authentic Greek cuisine
* Far fewer tourists than the famous islands

### Alternative destinations

* **Thassos** – crystal-clear water, pine forests, relaxed atmosphere, affordable.
* **Samothrace** – waterfalls, natural pools, hiking, almost untouched by mass tourism.
* **Milos** – beautiful volcanic landscapes and beaches, but still considerably calmer than Santorini.
